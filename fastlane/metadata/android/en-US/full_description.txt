Words Combination («Составь слова») is an offline Russian-language word game. Make new words using the letters of a longer word, complete challenges and collect stars.

Features:
- 15 levels grouped into six stages
- 49 challenges with different goals: word length, required letters, endings, riddles and more
- Step-by-step letter hints purchased with earned in-game units, not real money
- Local saving of words, level progress, stars, achievements and hints
- Eight types of level achievements, depending on available words and challenges
- Tap letter tiles, shuffle letters or switch to keyboard input
- Level completion results and animated stars
- Compact hints with task switching; familiar words and unseen targets take priority (repeats are possible once new targets run out)
- Dark theme follows the system; Start Over resets progress
- System phone-to-phone progress transfer on Android 12+, with no cloud backup; transfer is disabled on Android 7–11
- No play-time tracking
- No account, advertising, analytics or internet connection required

The interface and dictionary are currently in Russian. You can play entirely offline.

Gameplay data is stored on your device. The project's privacy policy explains local storage and deletion.
